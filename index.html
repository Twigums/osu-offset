Replay judgements

These counts come directly from the replay header and can differ from estimated head timing, especially for holds.

Method: replay key-down transitions are matched in order to beatmap note starts within the stable osu!mania 50 window. Hold releases are not evaluated; misses and timing judgements are estimates, not a full game-side resimulation. Notelock, unusual mods, and replay encoding variants can affect accuracy.
